# Short vertical

Under about 90 seconds, 9:16, watched on a phone, often without sound at first.

- **One idea.** When it needs "and also", it is two videos. Offer the second one in your note.
- **The first 2 seconds** hold a question, a claim, a result or a movement that raises one. No logo, no greeting, no slow pan. The first frame already shows something to look at.
- **Length.** As short as the idea allows, and inside what the brief says. 15 to 45 seconds suits most.
- **Frame.** 1080 by 1920. Wide footage has to be reframed: zoom until it fills the height (a 16:9 clip needs about 316%, so 4K footage stays sharp and 1080p doesn't), then place the subject with Pan. Look at the result at the start and the end of each shot with `view_frames`, since the subject moves and Pan doesn't follow. When the footage is too small, say so.
- **Phone buttons** cover the bottom fifth, the top tenth and the right edge. Keep faces, titles and anything to read in the middle.
- **Pace.** A change every 1 to 3 seconds: a cut, a size, a picture, a title. Cut every pause that doesn't mean something.
- **Without sound.** A title that says what it is about, in the first 2 seconds, when the picture doesn't. Captions only when the user or the rules ask for them.
- **Ending.** Stop on the point. A last frame that leads back to the first makes it loop.
