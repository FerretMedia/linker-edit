# Scripted, with a voice-over or lines to camera

The script leads. The pictures show what it says.

- **Match first.** Make a table in plan.md: each script line, the take that says it (file and times), and the picture that goes with it. Match on meaning, since people change words while they read. Compare with the transcript, not with your memory of it.
- **Missing lines.** When no take says a line, don't invent it. Leave it out when the rest still makes sense, or keep its place with a marker (`add_markers`) when it doesn't. List every missing line in your note. Say so plainly when a lot is missing.
- **Extra lines.** Something good that isn't in the script stays out, unless the brief leaves room. Mention it.
- **Takes.** The last complete take of a line, unless an earlier one is clearly better. Keep one voice and one distance to the microphone within a paragraph.
- **Voice first.** Lay the whole voice-over on A1 and tighten it, then place pictures on its words. A picture arrives on the word it shows, or up to half a second before.
- **Pictures.** Show the specific thing, not a general one. Two shots in a row of the same thing need a different size or angle. Use each shot once, unless it comes back on purpose.
- **Not enough pictures?** Hold a good one longer, or come back to the speaker. Don't fill with something that says something else. List what is missing so the user can shoot or find it.
- **Pace.** 2 to 5 seconds per picture, longer where the viewer has to read or look.
- **Pauses.** Give a paragraph break half a second. A list needs none.
