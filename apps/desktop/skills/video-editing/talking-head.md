# Talking head

One person to camera. The face and the words carry it, so the cut is mostly about words.

- **Opening.** The first 5 seconds say what the viewer gets, in the speaker's own words. The best line is often in the middle of the recording: move it to the front when it makes sense alone. Skip "hi, welcome back".
- **Order.** One point, then its example, then the next point. Move a part when it is told in the wrong order, but only between whole thoughts.
- **Takes.** Keep the last complete take of a line, unless an earlier one is clearly better: no stumble, more energy, the same meaning.
- **Pace.** A change in what the viewer sees every 5 to 15 seconds: a punch-in, B-roll, a title. Short videos change more often.
- **Jump cuts.** Hide each with B-roll, or change the size: alternate the shot's own zoom and about 1.1 to 1.2 times it (`set_clip_properties`, ZoomX and ZoomY, where 1 is the fitted size), and keep the eyes at the same height with Tilt. Two neighbouring shots at the same size and nothing on top look like a mistake. Never zoom past what the footage holds: 4K holds a zoom of 2 in a 1920 by 1080 timeline.
- **Punch-ins** come on the important line, not at random.
- **Ending.** Stop right after the last useful sentence, or after the call to action. No long goodbye.
